Four ways in. One standard.

Whether you are putting products on a shelf, in a lodge boutique, in a gift box or making them yourself, the filter is the same. Find the door that fits you.

01  ·  Hospitality & lodges

What does a guest physically carry home?

Guests do not travel to the Okavango or Chobe for something ordinary. They travel for rarity, exclusivity and story. Yet the retail products they meet in safari environments are usually imported, or generic curio.

A product sourced from Botswana, made with indigenous ingredients and modern quality standards, extends the experience past departure. It becomes a memory anchor, a conversation piece and a reinforcement of your brand.

Luxury travel now prioritises wellness, conscious consumption, authentic sourcing and credible ESG. A curated Botswana range strengthens your sustainability positioning and your community impact narrative at the same time, without either being the reason a guest buys.

What we supply

  • Wellness & in-room: natural room diffusers and sprays, motlopi tea, morula speciality products
  • Refined indulgence: low sugar bars, premium granola, dried fruits, roasted nut blends
  • Gourmet Botswana: morula juice, jam and sauces, artisanal marinades

These sit beside international luxury brands not because they are local, but because they meet the same standards.

02  ·  Retail & stockists

A wholesale arrangement with nothing clever in it.

We keep the commercial side deliberately plain, because complicated supply terms are how small local ranges die on a shelf.

  1. 01

    Clear pricing per line

    Defined wholesale pricing for each product, agreed up front, no moving targets.

  2. 02

    Samples before listing

    You see and handle the products before you commit shelf space to them.

  3. 03

    Direct invoicing

    One supplier, one invoice, instead of chasing twenty small producers separately.

  4. 04

    Consistent replenishment

    We hold the relationship with the maker so that reordering is our problem, not yours.

We do not compete on discount. We compete on quality, experience and narrative.

03  ·  Corporate gifting & pop-ups

Gifting that says something, and a pop-up that costs you nothing.

Hampers built to your budget. Tell us the spend per head and who is receiving it, and we will build a selection around it. Client gifts, staff recognition, conference packs, welcome boxes. Every item in the box is Botswana made and can be explained in one sentence, which is what makes a corporate gift memorable rather than polite.

Pop-up stores at your premises, at no cost to your organisation. We bring the stock, the stand and the staff. Your team gets convenient access to products they cannot easily find elsewhere, your organisation actively supports youth entrepreneurship, and it counts toward your CSR and employee engagement objectives.

We ran our first at FNB Botswana’s headquarters. It is flexible on dates and duration, and we work around whatever suits your calendar.

How a pop-up runs

  1. Agree a date

    A day or a week, whatever fits your building and your calendar.

  2. We set up

    Stock, stand and staff arrive and are in place before your team does.

  3. Your team shops

    Card and mobile payment on site. No admin lands on your people.

  4. We pack out

    We leave the space as we found it, and send you a short summary if you want one for CSR reporting.

04  ·  Producers & makers

You make it. We find it a shelf.

Most Botswana producers we meet are not held back by their product. They are held back by reach. Getting into a retailer, a lodge boutique or a corporate gift programme takes relationships, paperwork and persistence that a small maker rarely has spare capacity for.

That is the part we take on. We represent your product to buyers, handle the commercial conversation, hold the stock relationship and put you in rooms you would otherwise have to knock on for a year.

We are selective, and we say so plainly. Twenty producers have cleared the filter so far. We turn down more than we take, because that is what makes a buyer trust the next thing we put in front of them.

Before you get in touch

Check your product against the five criteria: refined packaging, clean ingredients, wellness aligned formulation, export ready labelling and the capacity to supply a second order as reliably as the first. If you are honest with yourself about all five, the conversation goes much faster.
