The part most people miss

Most people have never been shown what Botswana actually makes.

Cold pressed safflower oil, morula preserves, motlopi root coffee, hand poured diffusers, tissue oils, trail mixes. Made here, packaged properly, priced honestly.

The reason you have not seen them is rarely quality. It is distribution. Small producers make excellent things and then run out of road before those things reach a shelf, a lodge boutique or a gift box.

That gap is the entire reason JustBOTS exists.

The fact that these products are Botswana made is not the primary selling point. It is the added advantage.

Who we are

A sourcing and distribution company built around a filter, not a catalogue.

JustBOTS was founded by Precious Monnaanoka in September 2025. We have been growing steadily since.

We started with a pop-up store inside FNB Botswana’s headquarters, giving staff the chance to buy homegrown products without leaving the building. The response settled an argument we had been having internally. Demand was never the problem and neither was the quality. People had simply never been given the chance to see the products.

Since then we have supplied corporate clients, hosted pop-ups, built gift hampers to budget and listed a curated range online. The roster has grown to twenty producers. What has not changed is the filter. Everything we carry, most of it youth owned, has to sit beside an imported brand without needing an explanation.

Our curation standard

Five things a product has to be before we will carry it.

This list is the reason a buyer can say yes to us quickly. Every product we put in front of you has already been through it.

  1. 01

    Refined packaging

    It has to look like it belongs on the shelf it is going to. Packaging is the first quality signal a buyer reads, long before anyone tastes anything.

  2. 02

    Clean ingredient profile

    Natural ingredients, indigenous components where possible, minimal processing, reduced refined sugar and labelling that tells the truth.

  3. 03

    Wellness aligned

    Formulations that hold up against what conscious buyers now treat as the baseline rather than the bonus.

  4. 04

    Export ready

    Labelling, shelf life and compliance good enough to travel beyond Botswana, whether or not it ever does.

  5. 05

    Consistent supply

    A maker who can deliver the second order as reliably as the first. Reordering is where most local supply quietly falls apart.

If a product does not clear all five, we do not represent it. That is the whole policy.
